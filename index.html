The Hörppytila Vibe

Welcome to our living room. Kuuttila is not just a cocktail lounge; it's a neighborhood sanctuary for those who know. We focus on high-quality, handcrafted cocktails without the corporate stiffness.

Whether you're looking for an intimate corner, vibrant conversations, or our famous "hörppyjät" (sips), you'll find it here under the neon glow.
